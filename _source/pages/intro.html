I study how news media frame mental illness, and how those frames move audiences toward or away from seeking help. My research connects health and risk communication with media effects, with a focus on Ghana.

Before graduate school, I spent more than a decade as a news editor and national television anchor in Accra, and as the U.S. Embassy's media specialist for PEPFAR. I am on the 2026–27 academic job market and expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027.

Stephen Mante Anti

Ph.D. Candidate, Communication and Media Studies
School of Journalism and Communication, University of Oregon

I study how news media frame mental illness, and how those frames move audiences toward or away from seeking help. My research connects health and risk communication with media effects, with a focus on Ghana.

Before graduate school, I spent more than a decade as a news editor and national television anchor in Accra, and as the U.S. Embassy's media specialist for PEPFAR. I am currently on the job market and expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027.

Research

My dissertation, Framing Psychosis: News Media, Stigma, and the Mechanisms of Public Response in Ghana, pairs a content analysis of ten years of Ghanaian news coverage with an experiment that tests how fear and empathy frames shape stigma and the willingness to recommend help. Related work covers maternal mental health in Accra, contraceptive access across Africa, and depression on short-video platforms.
